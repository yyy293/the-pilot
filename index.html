SKYBOUND

A HIGH-ALTITUDE FLIGHT SIMULATOR

SELECT AIRCRAFT

Aircraft unlock as you climb higher and reach new speed milestones. Start with a light single-engine aircraft, then work your way toward high-performance jets and the Blackbird-class interceptor.

WORLD SETTINGS

Reach the upper atmosphere at around 100,000 ft and beyond. The sky darkens, the horizon curves, and the world falls away below you. This is a game-scale high-altitude experience, not a real orbital flight model.

AIRLINE & CAREER

Starting funds $25,000
Start as an independent pilot. Earn money from passenger and cargo contracts, buy aircraft, then establish your own airline for $15,000.
